---
title: Offline, on purpose
date: 2026-10-12
summary: Kotree now survives a dropped connection — cached pages, a proper offline fallback and a queue that delivers your secrets later.
---

A link-in-bio that shows a browser error page when the train goes into a tunnel is not much of a link-in-bio. So Kotree learned to cope: pages you have visited stay available, and unknown pages land on a friendly offline screen instead of the dinosaur.

The interesting part is what happens to your actions. Tap a link or leave a guestbook secret while offline, and it is queued on your device — then sent automatically the moment the connection returns, even if the tab is already closed.

No spinner, no lost secrets, no drama. Just the web behaving like it should have all along.
