Fourteen easter eggs are hidden across Kotree. Some are keyboard classics, some hide behind commands, and one only reveals itself if you are standing in the right place in the world.

The stats card on the home page now counts how many you have found, and the new [secrets page](/secrets) lists every one of them — with hints, not full spoilers, so the hunt stays fun.

Findings are stored on your device only. No accounts, no sync, no leaderboard — just you and the eggs.
