The long-form version of me: case studies of client work, personal projects and everything in between, with the full resume available as a PDF.

## Why a separate site

Kotree is the front door — one glance, every link. The portfolio is the deep end: the long stories, screenshots, the process behind each build, and the details a single card cannot hold.

## Stack notes

- Static-first pages with a lightweight Deno backend for dynamic bits
- Case studies written in Markdown so they age well
- The same neobrutalist design language as Kotree, without the easter eggs
